Welcome to Aura

Aura is a wallpaper app for your Mac. It plays static and animated wallpapers, builds a color theme from them, and helps your AI agent find new ones.

This installs Aura.app into your Applications folder. If an older Aura is running, it closes first.

When you first open it, Aura downloads a few wallpapers to start with. Your library lives in Pictures → Wallpapers.
